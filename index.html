05 — THE SUPERPARK WAY

Lessons From Our Team

“Our culture isn’t defined by what we say we believe in, but by what we choose to do.”

💜 A Team That Cares. A Team We Can Count On.

When one of our team members celebrated their engagement, everyone was invited, and naturally, everyone wanted to be there!

What made this moment special was how the team came together. Some adjusted their shifts to attend, while others stayed to keep the park running smoothly.

Together, they found a way to celebrate their colleague without compromising their responsibilities.

The Lesson?
True teamwork is about more than working together. It’s about caring for one another, supporting each other’s special moments, and knowing when to step up.

That’s a team we can count on.

💜 Ownership Is a Choice, and Attitude Makes the Difference.

One morning, the housekeeping cleaner didn’t show up. Instead of waiting for instructions or looking for someone to blame, the team on shift took action and cleaned the entire park together.

But what made this moment special wasn’t just what they did. It was how they chose to do it. With laughter, teamwork, and genuine care, they turned an unexpected challenge into a fun activity and a happy memory.

The Lesson?
Ownership isn’t about job titles. It’s about caring enough to act. And while we can’t always control what happens, we can always choose how we respond.

Two different moments. One shared culture.
That’s THE SUPERPARK WAY. 💜

08 — OUR FOCUS THIS MONTH

Know Our Top 4 Guests

The SuperPark Way: Every guest wants something different. Read them in the first 30 seconds.

WANTSMother: Peace of Mind
DOExplain the safety rules, show her where she can watch, check in on her during the visit.
SAY“Welcome! You can sit right here and see everything. I’m close by if you need me.”
WANTSChild: Excitement
DOMatch their energy, cheer, invite them in and celebrate their tricks.
SAY“Wow, great jump! Want to try the next one?”
WANTSTeenager: Freedom
DORespect them. Keep the rules short and clear. Never talk down to them.
SAY“Quick rules and then it’s all yours. Have fun!”
WANTSBirthday Family: Everything Perfect
DOKnow the child’s name, plan every step ahead and update the parents before they ask.
SAY“Happy birthday, [name]! Everything is ready. Let me show you the plan.”
ON-SHIFT CHALLENGE: Name the guest type in the first 30 seconds: smile, eye contact, welcome, offer help. And say goodbye to every family leaving your zone. HOW MANY NAMES CAN YOU REMEMBER?
